Milestone 22: file each DWAPI issue under the month it reports on

An NDWH DWAPI upload issue is stamped in the month AFTER the one it
reports on, and NDWH runs a month behind, so the previous issue is still
on disk when the next arrives and is still the only source for the older
reading. Scoring just the newest workbook therefore blanked a month and
put September's figure on the August tab.

Each issue is now scored on its own and filed under its reporting month:
the August issue (September stamps) gives 204 of 211 = 96.7% on the
Baseline tab, and the September issue (October stamps) gives 188 of 211
= 89.1% on the M1 September tab.

- _dwapi_workbooks globs every issue; _dwapi_coverages scores each.
- _prev_month_key derives the reporting month from the stamp month.
- _dwapi_coverage now accepts a workbook path instead of always reading
  the newest one, and reports reportingMonth/reportingMonthHuman.
- The M22 attach block matches a reading to a tab by the workbook's own
  month rather than newest-file-wins, and leaves the other tabs empty.
- The payload carries dwapiAll alongside dwapi (the newest issue).
- The front-end renders the reading for the tab it is opened from, and
  says so instead of guessing when no issue covers that month.
